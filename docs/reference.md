# hill reference

Generated by `hill-ops docs hill` from what hill declares; don't edit it here.

## Settings

Each with its `:set` name, what it does, its choices and its default.

### Layout

hill-ops · applies right away.

- **App width** (`layout.layout.app`): How much of the window's width the app takes, on the left, when it runs panes beside it. Choices: `15%`, `20%`, `25%`, `30%`, `35%`, `40%`, `45%`, `50%`. Default: `25%`.
- **Claude width** (`layout.layout.claude`): How much of the window's width the Claude pane takes, on the right of the app's panes; off hides it. Choices: `off`, `15%`, `20%`, `25%`, `30%`, `35%`, `40%`, `45%`, `50%`. Default: `30%`.
- **Panel height** (`layout.panel_height`): How much of the window this panel covers. Choices: `1/4`, `1/3`, `1/2`. Default: `1/3`.

### Hill

hill-ops · applies right away.

- **Group tabs** (`hill.tabs`): Show the group tabs above the settings, or below them. Choices: `top`, `bottom`. Default: `top`.
- **Clock** (`hill.clock`): The time at the strip's right end: in the locale's format, 12-hour, 24-hour, or off. On a narrow strip it goes before the app's keys do. Choices: `locale`, `12-hour`, `24-hour`, `off`. Default: `locale`.
- **Event log** (`hill.events`): Keep a month of commands, settings and help you use, for Claude; never your text. Choices: `false` (off), `true` (on). Default: `true`.
- **Claude's tips** (`hill.claude_tips`): When Claude offers tips: always in the panel, and once a session on the strip's line; only when asked; or never. Questions work either way. Choices: `once a session` (always), `on Alt-c` (only when asked), `off`. Default: `once a session`.
- **Claude's model** (`hill.claude_model`): The model for Claude in the strip: sonnet, the faster haiku, or opus. Choices: `sonnet`, `haiku`, `opus`. Default: `sonnet`.

## Commands

Typed after `:` on the panel's line.

- `:set SETTING VALUE`: change a setting: SETTING is its `:set` name, VALUE one of its choices.
- `:toggle SETTING`: change a setting to its next value, round to the first.

## Keys

### Strip

- `click`: run a hint on the strip
- `Esc`: close what the strip shows and go back to the app; so does a click on the app
- `:`: panel's line: a command, which the app runs; anything else searches, and Ret asks Claude
- `↓ Tab`: panel's line, with matches shown: pick one, which Ret takes: a setting or a key shows, a command is typed
- `key tree`: a key goes down a group or runs its command, as a click on it does; Bksp goes up a level
- `:set :toggle`: panel's line: change a setting to a value, or to its next one, GROUP.KEY completed with Tab
- `Tab`: panel's line, after ":": complete the highlighted command
- `↑ ↓`: panel's line, after ":": pick a command; else between the settings and the line
- `Ret`: panel's line: run the command, ask Claude, or take what it offers; question: answer it
- `PgUp PgDn`: panel: scroll Claude's answer
- `drag a border`: resize the panes beside it; the widths snap to Layout's steps, and are kept
- `Alt-c`: panel: another tip from Claude, as a click on the tip does; help: a tip about what's shown
